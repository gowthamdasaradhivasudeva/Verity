Q1: What is Artificial Intelligence ?
Ans: Artificial intelligence is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
AI Systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, and make decisions.

Q2: What is Machine learning ?
Ans: Machine learning is a part of artificial intelligence. It allows computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed for every task.
There are 3 main types of ML :
1. Supervised learning
2. Unsupervised learning
3. Reinforcement learning.
